What It Solves

Make delivery oversight structured, visible, and repeatable.

PMO modernization is not about adding ceremony. It is about creating the right operating structure so leaders can see risk, make decisions, and keep work moving.

Common PMO friction

  • Intake and prioritization are inconsistent across teams or programs.
  • Risk, issue, action, and dependency tracking is fragmented or stale.
  • Executive updates require manual consolidation and last-minute cleanup.
  • Decision-making and change control are not documented cleanly.

What AAS delivers

  • PMO operating model with governance roles and cadence.
  • Intake, prioritization, and change control structure.
  • Risk, assumptions, issues, and dependencies log.
  • Integrated master schedule structure and delivery health dashboard.
  • Executive status template, decision log, and PMO playbook.
