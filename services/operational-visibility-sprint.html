What It Solves

Replace status chasing with a system leadership can actually use.

The visibility sprint standardizes how work is captured, owned, aged, escalated, and reported so leaders can manage execution in real time.

Visibility gaps addressed

  • Work is tracked in too many places with no shared operating view.
  • Leadership cannot easily see what is late, stuck, high-risk, or ownerless.
  • Status meetings produce updates, but not reliable operational signal.
  • Approvals and decisions are not visible until they become blockers.

What AAS builds

  • Future-state visibility model and status definitions.
  • Centralized workflow tracker with ownership, due dates, aging, and priority fields.
  • Executive dashboard for active work, risk, approvals, workload, and decisions.
  • Weekly reporting template and governance cadence.
  • User guide and handoff materials for sustained use.
