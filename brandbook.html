LogoAAS brand guide

Logo

A precise, ascending monogram paired with a clean geometric wordmark. It signals structure, intelligence, and forward motion — and stays legible from a favicon to a building façade.

Logo color useAAS brand guide

Logo color use

Use the ink lockup on light fields and the reversed lockup on charcoal, blue, or slate. Preserve clear space, keep contrast high, and never recolor or distort the wordmark.
